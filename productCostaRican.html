Costa Rican Tarrazu

$20.99 / 12 oz bag

Our Costa Rican Tarrazu coffee comes from the renowned Tarrazu region in Costa Rica, known for producing some of the finest coffees in Central America. With its rich soil and ideal altitude, this coffee offers a bright, clean, and flavorful cup with a perfect balance of sweetness and acidity.

Light roasted to preserve its vibrant character, this coffee features bright citrus and tropical fruit notes, along with a mild, balanced body and a crisp finish. Ideal for those who enjoy a smooth, yet flavorful cup with a lively profile.

Product Details

  • Origin: Costa Rica (Tarrazu region)
  • Altitude: 1,200-1,800 meters
  • Process: Washed
  • Roast Level: Light
  • Tasting Notes: Citrus, Tropical Fruit, Bright Acidity
  • Body: Light to Medium
  • Acidity: Crisp and Bright
